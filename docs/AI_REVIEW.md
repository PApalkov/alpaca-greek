# AI review: essays, letters, role-plays

Everything a teacher would normally check is checked by ChatGPT or Claude. The app prepares the request and keeps the results.

## Where it happens

- **Lessons of type `task` and `test`:**
  - an `essay` step: you write the text in the app and send it for review;
  - a `speak` step: a role-play with the AI, by voice or text.
- **Course → «Свой текст на проверку»:** any text of your own, such as homework, a message or a diary entry.

## The loop

1. **Write** in the app. The text is saved, and the word counter shows the required length.
2. **Send.** «Скопировать запрос» copies a ready prompt; «Открыть в ChatGPT» or «Открыть в Claude» opens a new chat with the prompt already typed in, and also copies it in case the link drops it. The prompt contains:
   - your level and the context (Russian speaker in Cyprus, Standard Modern Greek, the Ελληνομάθεια exam);
   - the task, its requirements and the length;
   - your text;
   - the answer format: the corrected text, an error table, a score by the exam criteria (task completion, vocabulary, grammar, coherence: 0–5 each, out of 20), strengths, what to revise, and a native-like rewrite of two sentences;
   - a JSON block at the end with the score and up to 10 mistakes.
3. **For a role-play:** in the ChatGPT or Claude app on the phone, paste the prompt and switch to voice mode. The AI plays its role at your level and does not interrupt with corrections. Say «τέλος» to end, and it steps out of the role and gives the review.
4. **Paste the whole answer back** into «Вставь ответ ИИ сюда» and press «Сохранить разбор». The app:
   - saves the review and the score (you can see them in the lesson, and in «Свой текст» for your own texts);
   - turns every mistake from the JSON block into a personal exercise «Исправь свою ошибку». It shows your wrong phrase, you type the corrected one, and the rule is explained after you answer;
   - schedules these exercises in the Leitner box. They come into the daily warm-up, and «Мои ошибки» (on the Course and Grammar pages) lists them and drills them.

If the answer has no JSON block (the AI forgot it), the review is still saved, but no exercises are created. Ask the AI: «Добавь, пожалуйста, json-блок из запроса».

## Tips

- Write without a dictionary first. Look words up only after the first draft, then send.
- Paste the review back the same day: the mistakes are freshest then.
- Take the review prompt's «что повторить» seriously. The matching grammar topic is in «Грамматика», and the epoch's practice lessons cover it again.
- For speaking, record yourself or use voice mode. Reading your answers aloud from the screen trains reading, not speaking.
